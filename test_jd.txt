Job Title: Senior Backend Engineer
Location: Remote
Company: CloudSync Tech

About Us
CloudSync Tech is a fast-growing startup revolutionizing cloud storage solutions for enterprise clients. We are looking for an experienced Senior Backend Engineer to join our core product team.

Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain scalable, reliable, and highly available backend services and APIs.
- Work closely with product managers and frontend engineers to define feature requirements and technical specifications.
- Lead the architecture transition towards a containerized microservices infrastructure.
- Optimize application performance and database queries for maximum speed and scalability.
- Implement robust security measures and data protection protocols.
- Mentor and guide junior developers on best practices and system design.

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional backend software development experience.
- Strong proficiency in Python (FastAPI/Django) or Go.
- Solid experience designing and optimizing relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL).
- Hands-on experience with cloud platforms, preferably AWS (EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda).
- Proficiency with containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Deep understanding of RESTful API design principles and microservices architecture.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.

Nice to Have
- Experience with real-time data streaming (Kafka, RabbitMQ).
- Knowledge of infrastructure as code (Terraform, Ansible).
- Familiarity with NoSQL databases (MongoDB, Redis).
